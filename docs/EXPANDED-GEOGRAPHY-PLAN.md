# Expanded geography — provisional plan

Expand to a 142 × 142 grid of 80 m cells: 11.36 km per side, about 49.83 square miles. Keep one main town and two or three outlying suburbs. Support compact town, avenue settlement and twin-center town motifs, with district placement and roads following the chosen structure.

Each cell should describe a mixture, not one universal terrain label. Sample 25 subcells (16 m squares), record terrain percentages summing to 100%, and aggregate elevation, moisture, flood risk and roughness. Terrain includes river, lake, woods, meadow, hills, marsh, scrub, rock, cropland and built ground. Correlated noise, meandering hydrology, irregular development boundaries and terrain-guided countryside should avoid the old straight woodland/farmland split. Sample resolution is a modeling choice; percentages are known area within the abstraction, not a real survey.

Continue connected roads and playable object-specific scavenging. Expose map layers and cell inspection. Fractional water requires explicit bridge metadata even where water is not the dominant cover. Preserve the old generator and old saves; save actual new geography and validate its composition. Terrain/equipment and damaged/off-road route rules remain future gameplay, but their inputs must exist and be inspectable now.

Acceptance: deterministic ~50-square-mile maps; three distinct motifs across seeds and selectable for inspection; populated detached suburbs; coherent district/road/site arrangement; rich irregular terrain and mixed boundary cells; valid fractions, bounds, bridges, routes, identity and travel timing; old generated save compatibility; new save/load/replay and mass conservation; real desktop input and rendered representative maps.
